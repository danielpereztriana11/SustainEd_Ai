# SustainEd AI 🌱

Ask a question about sustainability and get an answer in four parts: a **definition**, an **example** in your business, a practical **action** and a **check** question to test yourself.

Open the chat settings next to the message box to choose your level (beginner, intermediate or advanced) and your business.

The answers come from `microsoft/Phi-3-mini-4k-instruct` fine-tuned with LoRA, served by a Vertex AI endpoint. This is an educational assistant: it does not give legal or regulatory advice, and its answers can be wrong or out of date.
